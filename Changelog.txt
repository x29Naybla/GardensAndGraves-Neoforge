changelog:
* planter interaction sounds are now dynamically chosen from the substrate being taken from/placed into it
* made each plant have their own tag to dictate what types of substrate supports them

* now the entire dirt tag is included as valid planter substrate instead of only dirt and mycelium
* potted plants now store the item used to pot them, instead of being defined to always return a Flower Pot
* added support for Arts & Crafts dyed flower pots
* changed the planter_substrates tag to have the vanilla smelts_to_glass tag instead of passing Sand and Red Sand
* changed the watering cans to check the neoforge villager_farmlands tag instead of hardcoding the vanilla farmland
* removed Enderman from the drops_brain tag
* removed the endermen tag
* readded mod support; additions to the drops_brain tag once again include:
* * Guard villager's guards,
* * Mobs of Mythology's kobolds and kobold warriors
* * Touhou Little Maid's maids and maid fairies
* * Raided's illagers
* * Radical Cobblemon Trainers' trainers
